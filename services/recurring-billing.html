Recurring Billing FAQ

Answers regarding subscription logic, prorations, and customer self-service.

When a subscriber upgrades or downgrades their plan mid-billing cycle, PayFlowPro calculates the exact seconds remaining in the period. It credits the unused portion of the old tier and charges only the prorated remainder of the new tier, either immediately or as a credit adjustment on their next invoice.
PayFlowPro connects directly with Visa Account Updater (VAU) and Mastercard Automatic Billing Updater (ABU). When a customer's bank reissues an expired card or replaces a lost card, the network securely updates the stored token with the new expiration date and card number without interrupting service.
