Fraud Protection FAQ

Questions regarding liability shift, false decline prevention, and dispute automation.

When an online transaction is authenticated via 3DS 2.3, card network rules stipulate that if the cardholder later claims the charge was unauthorized, the issuing bank is legally responsible for the loss rather than the merchant. This completely shields your business from fraudulent chargeback deductions.
Rather than applying crude rule blocks (e.g., blocking all international cards or VPNs), our models evaluate holistic behavioral telemetry. Genuine shoppers traveling abroad or using privacy relays pass smoothly, keeping our false positive decline rate below 0.05% of orders.
When a buyer falsely claims they didn't make a purchase ("friendly fraud"), Visa CE3.0 allows merchants to provide two prior undisputed transactions from that same cardholder with matching IP, device fingerprint, or delivery address. PayFlowPro's gateway automatically pulls this historical telemetry and submits it, invalidating the dispute before chargeback penalties are assessed.
