HIPAA-Compliant Gateway

Streamline Patient Collections While Safeguarding Protected Health Information

Collecting patient co-pays, deductibles, and out-of-pocket costs shouldn't burden front-desk staff. PayFlowPro's healthcare architecture provides secure card tokenization, pre-authorized treatment payment plans, and zero raw PAN storage on clinic hardware.

  • Dedicated Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with every medical merchant account
  • Auto-detection and low-cost routing for Health Savings Account (HSA) & Flexible Spending (FSA) cards
  • SMS text and email payment links allowing patients to pay securely from their own devices
  • Flexible installment payment schedules for high-deductible surgical and dental procedures
