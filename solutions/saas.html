SaaS & Subscription Solutions FAQ

Answers to recurring billing and churn prevention questions.

When a customer's credit card expires or is reported lost, issuing banks push updated PANs and expiration dates to Visa VAU and Mastercard ABU networks. PayFlowPro syncs directly with these clearinghouses to update your stored tokens automatically without customer outreach.

Your backend streams raw telemetry events (API requests, compute minutes, active seats, gigabytes stored) to our idempotent ingestion pipeline. PayFlowPro aggregates units over the billing month, applies your custom tier or graduated volume pricing, and automatically generates an accurate, itemized invoice at billing cut-off.

Subscribers receive a secure, white-labeled portal where they can update payment cards or SEPA/ACH accounts, upgrade or downgrade tiers, switch between monthly and annual plans, add billing contacts, and download past itemized PDF invoices with compliant VAT and sales tax breakdowns.

When a customer switches tiers mid-billing cycle, the engine calculates the exact unused balance of the prior plan down to the second and credits it toward the new tier. You can elect to invoice the prorated difference immediately or apply it as an adjustment on the next scheduled renewal invoice.

We provide signed, replay-protected webhooks for all events, including `subscription.created`, `invoice.payment_succeeded`, `invoice.payment_failed`, `customer.subscription.updated`, and `subscription.canceled`, allowing your core software to grant or revoke app access immediately.
