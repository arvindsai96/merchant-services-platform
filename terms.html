Section 01

1. Merchant Account & Fast Underwriting

By submitting an application or registering an account on PayFlowPro, you ("Merchant", "You", or "Business") represent that you are an authorized representative of the legal entity named during enrollment and agree to be bound by this Merchant Processing Agreement.

  • Identity Verification (KYB/KYC): All merchant applications undergo automated next-day underwriting verification, including TIN/EIN verification, beneficial ownership checks, and bank account validation.
  • Truthfulness of Information: The Merchant warrants that all business details, expected monthly card volumes, and merchant category codes (MCC) are accurate and kept current.
  • Provisional Acceptance: Initial account activation is conditional upon complete document review and sponsor bank acquiring clearance.
Section 02

2. Payment Processing & Card Schemes

PayFlowPro provides omnichannel acquiring connectivity for Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, ACH Direct Debit, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and contactless smart terminal devices.

The Merchant agrees to honor all valid credit, debit, and prepaid cards without discrimination in accordance with Visa Core Rules, MasterCard Bylaws, and American Express Merchant Regulations.

The Merchant may only submit payment transactions resulting directly from bona fide sales of goods or services provided directly by the Merchant to the customer.

Section 03

3. Interchange-Plus Fees & Transparent Billing

PayFlowPro operates on a pure Interchange-Plus pricing architecture, ensuring complete fee transparency without hidden surcharges or synthetic tier downgrades.

  • Pass-Through Interchange: Direct wholesale assessment rates published by card networks are passed through at net cost.
  • Contract Terms: No long-term lock-in or cancellation penalties apply for standard month-to-month merchant agreements.
  • Automated Fee Deduction: Processing fees, gateway costs, and chargeback handling fees are deducted daily from the gross batch settlement prior to bank disbursement.
Section 04

4. Settlements, Payouts & Reserves

Net approved transaction funds are batched daily and deposited via ACH to your verified business checking account according to the standard settlement schedule (typically Next Business Day by 7:00 AM EST).

  • Cut-off Windows: Transactions authorized before 11:00 PM EST qualify for next-day bank settlement.
  • Risk Reserves: For high-risk classifications, seasonal spikes, or elevated chargeback velocities, PayFlowPro reserves the right to establish a rolling risk reserve (5%–10%) to secure chargeback obligations.
  • ACH Recourse: In the event of negative merchant balance or excessive refunds, PayFlowPro is authorized to debit the linked deposit account.
Section 05

5. Chargebacks, Inquiries & Disputes

Merchants are directly responsible for all chargebacks, reversals, and associated card scheme dispute fees incurred on their account.

Dispute Threshold:< 0.9% of monthly transactions
Evidence Submission Window:12 Calendar Days

Our automated Chargeback Shield provides pre-dispute alerts (Ethoca / Verifi) allowing merchants to issue rapid refunds prior to formal chargeback filing.

Section 06

6. PCI-DSS Level 1 & Security Duties

PayFlowPro maintains full PCI-DSS Level 1 Service Provider compliance with tokenized card storage, Point-to-Point Encryption (P2PE), and TLS 1.3 encryption.

  • No Raw PAN Storage: Merchants must never store unencrypted Primary Account Numbers (PAN), CVV/CVC security codes, or PIN verification data on local servers.
  • Annual SAQ Validation: Merchants must complete their relevant Self-Assessment Questionnaire (SAQ A / SAQ A-EP) annually via the Merchant Portal.
  • API Key Custody: Live secret API keys and webhook signing tokens must remain confidential and never committed to public code repositories.
Section 07

7. Prohibited Businesses & Termination

PayFlowPro strictly prohibits payment processing for unlawful goods, unauthorized pharmaceuticals, unlicensed gambling, deceptive marketing schemes, and intellectual property infringement.

Either party may terminate this agreement at any time with 30 days written notice. Immediate termination may occur upon material breach, fraud, excessive chargebacks, or sponsor bank mandate.
