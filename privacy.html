Privacy Policy

  2. Privacy Policy
Last Updated: October 2026
GDPR & CCPA Compliant PCI-DSS Level 1
Section 01

1. Information We Collect

PayFlowPro collects information to deliver secure omnichannel merchant services, process financial transactions, verify merchant identities (KYC/KYB), and comply with federal banking regulations.

  • Merchant Business Information: Legal business name, Tax Identification Number (EIN/TIN), business registration documents, authorized signatory information, and corporate bank account details.
  • Transaction Information: Transaction amounts, timestamps, card brand, currency, issuing bank location, and anonymized order reference IDs.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: IP address, device telemetry, browser type, and anti-fraud fraud risk scoring signals.
Section 02

2. Payment & Tokenized Data

We maintain end-to-end tokenization and point-to-point encryption (P2PE) across all payment channels. Sensitive payment instrument credentials (such as Primary Account Numbers or CVV security codes) are never stored in raw readable format on our servers or merchant systems.

All payment credentials are encrypted using AES-256 and stored inside our certified PCI-DSS Level 1 secure token vault.
Section 03

3. How We Use Information

We use the collected information for the following legitimate business and legal purposes:

  • Facilitating credit card, debit card, ACH, and digital wallet authorizations and settlements.
  • Underwriting merchant risk, assessing financial integrity, and preventing money laundering (AML).
  • Operating real-time machine learning fraud screening and 3D Secure 2.3 verification.
  • Generating transaction reconciliation reports and Interchange-Plus statement audits.
Section 04

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

PayFlowPro does not sell merchant or customer personal data to advertisers or third-party brokers. We disclose payment data only to:

  • Payment Schemes & Acquiring Banks: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, and sponsor member banks necessary to route settlement funds.
  • Regulatory & Law Enforcement Authorities: When required by court subpoena, anti-money laundering reporting, or applicable banking regulations.
  • Security & Anti-Fraud Partners: Certified fraud prevention networks (such as Ethoca and Verifi) strictly for pre-dispute deflection.
Section 05

5. Data Retention & Security

Transaction records and underwriting dossiers are retained in accordance with financial regulatory requirements (typically 5 to 7 years to satisfy federal record-keeping and audit mandates). We enforce TLS 1.3 transmission encryption, multi-factor administrative authentication, and round-the-clock SOC 2 monitored perimeter defenses.

Section 06

6. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR in Europe and CCPA in California), you have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, rectification, portability, or deletion of personal data not required for ongoing legal and tax compliance.

For any privacy questions or data subject requests, please email us directly at privacy@payflowpro.com or visit our Contact Support desk.
