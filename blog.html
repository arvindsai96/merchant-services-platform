Domain 01: Payment Processing

Wholesale Interchange Transparency & B2B Level 3 Savings

The card processing fee stack consists of three distinct layers: Network Interchange paid to the cardholder's issuing bank, Card Brand Assessment fees (Visa/Mastercard/Amex), and Processor markup. Most traditional processors pad these fees inside opaque "tiered" buckets.

  • Interchange-Plus Pass-Through: Direct routing at true network wholesale rates with zero hidden markups on regulated debit.
  • Automated Level 3 Ingestion: Ingesting line-item commodity codes and customer tax IDs to reduce corporate card interchange by 1.15%.
  • Same-Day ACH Acceleration: 11:00 PM EST late-batch settlement cutoff guaranteeing next-morning capital liquidity.

Financial Lexicon

Merchant Payment Glossary & Economic Terms

Essential financial concepts, regulatory terminology, and card acquiring definitions every CFO, controller, and payments architect should know.

Interchange-Plus
Pricing Model

A transparent pricing structure where the merchant pays the exact wholesale interchange rate set by Visa/Mastercard plus a disclosed, fixed processor markup.

Basis Point (bps)
Financial Unit

One-hundredth of one percentage point (0.01%). In merchant processing agreements, a processor markup of 15 bps represents exactly 0.15% on gross sales.

Chargeback Ratio
Risk Metric

Total monthly disputes divided by total transaction count. Card networks place merchants exceeding 0.90% into mandatory excessive chargeback monitoring programs.

Compelling Evidence 3.0
Visa Rule

Visa framework allowing merchants to dismiss friendly fraud disputes immediately by demonstrating two prior undisputed transactions from the same cardholder.

Network Tokenization
Security Tech

Replacing Primary Account Numbers (PANs) with cryptographic tokens issued directly by Visa VTS and Mastercard MDES, boosting authorization rates and domain security.

Point-to-Point Encryption
PCI-P2PE

Immediate hardware encryption of card data inside terminal pin pads, ensuring raw card details never touch local POS software or merchant network servers.

Batch Settlement Cutoff
Treasury

The designated daily cutoff time (e.g. 11:00 PM EST) by which transactions must be batched to guarantee deposit into operating bank accounts the next business morning.

Smart Dunning
SaaS Churn

Machine-learning retry logic that strategically schedules recurring subscription billing attempts on dates with historically high issuer liquidity (e.g. 1st and 15th).

Level 3 Processing
B2B Commercial

Enhanced invoice line-item data (PO numbers, commodity codes, tax amounts) submitted during clearing that lowers commercial card interchange by up to 1.15%.

Dual Pricing
POS Program

A compliant payment model clearly displaying cash versus card pricing, allowing merchants to offset 100% of processing overhead while remaining within network rules.

Acquiring Bank
Banking Rail

The registered financial institution that underwrites merchant risk, provides merchant accounts, and routes card transaction funds through the card network clearing houses.

Hosted Fields (iFrame)
Developer API

Sandboxed HTML inputs hosted on PCI Level 1 servers embedded in merchant checkout pages, isolating card numbers from merchant server memory to qualify for SAQ-A.
