The Transaction Flow

What Happens Behind Every Swipe, Dip & Click

From the split-second a customer presents their card to the final settlement in your checking account, PayFlowPro executes a 4-stage automated clearing pipeline in under 300 milliseconds:

1
Contactless & EMV Chip Capture

Cardholder data is read via EMV Contact chip, NFC (Apple Pay / Google Pay), or 256-bit hosted web fields. The primary card number is immediately converted into an irreversible cryptographic token.

2
AI Fraud & 3D Secure 2.3 Screening

In under 40 milliseconds, our machine learning engine screens 120+ risk indicators (device fingerprint, IP reputation, velocity, geolocation) to approve legitimate buyers and stop fraudulent disputes.

3
Direct Card Scheme Clearing

The transaction bypasses third-party reseller gateways, routing directly through Visa, Mastercard, Amex, or Discover interchange rails straight to the cardholder's issuing bank for immediate approval.

4
Automated Daily Batch Settlement

Transactions close automatically in your daily batch at 11:00 PM EST and deposit straight into your commercial bank account via FedACH within 24 hours, complete with line-item interchange audit reporting.
